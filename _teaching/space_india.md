Worked as an Educator in SPACE India for one year from November 2023. Job requirements involve teaching astronomical concepts in Schools in and around Chennai, and conducting telescope observation sessions for the students. I have the experience handling a Dobsonian Telescope and conducting overnight sessions.
I also have experience conducting public astronomical outreach events and content marketing for the company. I also learnt the basics of Astrophotography and developed a passion for astropics!
